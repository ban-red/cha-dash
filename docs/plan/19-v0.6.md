# 19 · v0.6: Toolkit, the last release before 1.0's betas

> **Status: agreed** (2026-11-06). The maintainer settled [K1–K9](#open-questions-k1k9) as recommended ([Decisions](#decisions)). Builds on 0.5 ([15](15-v0.5.md), [16](16-stacks-parity.md)) and 0.5.1 ([18](18-v0.5.1.md)). The roadmap goes from v0.5 to v1.0 ([04](04-roadmap.md#v10-home)). v0.6 takes 1.0's remaining features, plus everything 15 and 16 let slip to "v0.5.x", so 1.0 has only guarantees, the review, translations and distribution left, and its betas change no behaviour.

**House rule (from [17](17-test-automation.md#the-rule)):** nothing is tested by hand. Every check is an automated test the agent writes and runs, on throwaway data directories, containers and registries. The lab is never a test target: it's only read, by `live:verify`, to check fixtures. Dev servers and builds still need the maintainer's say. **Writing to the lab is an operation, not a test, and needs a go-ahead per run against named targets.**

**The goal:** nothing a homelabber does day to day needs another tool open beside Cha Dash. A shell in a container, a full disk of old images, a router's gateways, a Synology's volumes, a Prometheus alert and a feed of release notes are all on the board or one action away.

**Demo moment:** a Prometheus alert wakes the wall display; on a phone, the container's sheet shows the disk filling with old images, Prune frees 12 GB with the plan saying so, and a shell in the container confirms the fix.

## Docker host

From [16](16-stacks-parity.md#design), sections 5 and 6, settled as S3 and S6.

### A shell in a container

- **Transport (S3):** input frames on the existing call, hub → node, beside the output frames node → hub. `nodeproto` gets a version bump; a node too old for exec refuses it and the sheet says to update the node.
- **Policy:** the node's `docker.exec` grant and its `exec: true` switch, both off by default. `docker.protect` refuses exec too. The hub's action is T2, operators and up. Each shell is a step-up session of its own, ending after 15 minutes idle and 2 hours in all ([K2](#open-questions-k1k9)).
- **Audit:** who opened a shell in what, when it ended and how. Not the keystrokes: a shell's input holds passwords, and an audit log full of them is worse than none.
- **Terminal (S6):** our own small component, loaded only when a shell opens, so the initial-JS budget doesn't move. Line editing, colours, copy and paste, resize. Full-screen programs can slip to v0.6.x; if the component passes ~25 kB gzipped to reach them, that's an ADR.
- **Security review of its own**, before it ships: frame injection, a shell outliving its session, resize and control sequences, a container whose output floods the socket.

### Images, volumes and networks

As 16's design 6: new `docker.read` verbs `image.list`, `volume.list` and `network.list`, each with what uses it; a node's page lists them, with size and "used by".

- **Prune images:** dangling only by default, "all unused" as an option, T2, with the space it frees in the plan. A new grant, `docker.prune`, off by default.
- **Volumes are never pruned in bulk.** One unused volume can be removed, T3, typed to confirm. The plan says whether backup coverage saw a backup of it, and which stack last used it if the labels tell.
- **Networks:** list only.

## Sources

Prefer a spec unless the API needs Go (see `internal/README.md`). Each ships a recipe, fixtures recorded from a throwaway instance where one runs ([18](18-v0.5.1.md#fixtures-from-real-instances)), and a contract test.

| Source | What | Tier |
|---|---|---|
| **Prometheus and Alertmanager** | Firing alerts as incidents, with their labels and severity mapped to Cha Dash's levels; a tile showing one PromQL query as a stat, a gauge or a chart ([K4](#open-questions-k1k9)) | Go: PromQL results and Alertmanager's grouping don't fit a spec's extract |
| **OPNsense, wider** | Gateways and their loss and latency, firmware updates waiting (into the Update Center, read only), firewall and service health. Today's WAN stays | Go, in `integrations/opnsense` |
| **Synology DSM** | Volumes, storage pools and disks with SMART; Hyper Backup tasks feed backup coverage like PBS; packages and their updates ([K5](#open-questions-k1k9)) | Go: DSM's session login and its per-API version discovery |
| **Feeds** | RSS and Atom: a Feed widget; and in the Update Center, a project's release feed beside its update, fetched by the hub ([K6](#open-questions-k1k9)) | Go, Tier 0, like `ical` |

**Home Assistant proposes** ([K8](#open-questions-k1k9)). ha-chadash adds a service, `chadash.propose`, that proposes a Cha Dash action with an agent token. It goes through the same approval as an agent's proposal over MCP: it shows in the Inbox, and a person approves it with a passkey. HA can't approve its own proposal, and a proposal at T3 or above names the HA automation that made it. Tests: the hub's side in the agents' tests, and the HA side in ha-chadash's CI, which loads HA in a container ([18](18-v0.5.1.md#home-assistant-loading-the-integration)).

## Displays

What wave 33 left out ([15](15-v0.5.md#wave-33-how-displays-work)).

- **Rotation** ([K7](#open-questions-k1k9)): a display names one or more boards and how long each shows. Each board keeps its own scope: the socket serves the union of what the display's boards show, and a board's tiles see only their board's. Waking for an incident shows the board that holds it.
- **A schedule per display:** quiet hours today are one window. A schedule allows a window per day of the week, and which board shows when (the kitchen's morning board, the evening one).
- **Camera pictures** ([K9](#open-questions-k1k9)): the picture route accepts a display's credential only for a camera a tile on one of its boards shows, at the tile's resolution, at most every 10 seconds. Anything else answers 401, as now.

Tests: the scope union and the picture refusals in `TestDisplays` and the authz matrix; rotation and the schedule in `lib/display.ts`'s fake-time tests and in 18's week-on-a-display spec.

## Config in git

1.0 promises git sync for config ([04](04-roadmap.md#v10-home)). Stacks already sync from git ([ADR-0021](../adr/0021-git-sync-and-deploy-webhooks.md)). Config is different: the hub is where it's edited, and secrets must never reach the repository.

- **One way, hub to git** ([K3](#open-questions-k1k9)): each change to a board, a connection, a policy, a display or a notification rule is a commit in a repository the admin names, as a YAML file per object. Secret fields are written as their `${secret:…}` references, never values. Display secrets and API tokens aren't written at all.
- **Restore reads it back:** a new hub pointed at the repository and at the export bundle's vault (or asked for each secret) recreates the config. Tested as an upgrade test is: a data directory written from the repository matches the one that wrote it.
- **An ADR** for the file layout and what's excluded, since it becomes part of 1.0's stable config.

## Toward 1.0

v0.6 does the groundwork so 1.0's betas only harden:

- **Spec `v1` drafted:** what `v1beta1` taught the community repository, written as a schema beside `v1beta1`, with `chadash spec migrate` to it, behind a flag. Frozen in 1.0.
- **Translations ready:** every user-facing string through a message ID, a test that fails on a literal string in a component, and an English catalogue Weblate can read. Weblate itself is 1.0.
- **The security review's scope:** the threat model updated for exec, Home Assistant proposing, config in git and display rotation, and the review's brief written, so it can start as 1.0's first beta ships.
- **Upgrades:** `testdata/upgrade/0.5.0` exists; 0.6 adds its own folder when it's cut, as each release does.

## Done when

- A shell opens in a container through the node, in the browser, under `e2e:system`; a node without `docker.exec` refuses it; `docker.protect` refuses it; a shell closes at its idle and total limits on the test clock; the audit has its start and end and no input. Exec's security review has no open finding above low.
- Prune images frees what its plan said (engine test, measured), and a volume a backup never covered says so in its plan.
- Prometheus: an Alertmanager alert raises an incident and resolves it, from a throwaway Prometheus and Alertmanager in an engine test; a PromQL tile renders at all four levels.
- OPNsense, Synology and feeds pass their contract tests. Synology's fixtures are recorded from DSM where a test instance exists, and `live:verify` covers them otherwise.
- A Home Assistant automation's proposal shows in the Inbox and runs only after a passkey approval, in ha-chadash's CI against a demo-mode hub.
- A display rotates between two boards and never serves a tile from a board it doesn't name (authz matrix); a camera on its board shows, one off it is refused.
- A config repository restores a hub whose boards, connections and policies match the original, and holds no secret (a test greps the repository for every secret the vault holds).
- Spec `v1` migrates every bundled spec and the community repository's, behind its flag.
- No component has a literal user-facing string.
- The changelog has each user-facing change under `## [Unreleased]`.

## Open questions (K1–K9)

| # | Question | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| K1 | Is v0.6 the last minor before 1.0? | Yes. After 0.6, 1.0's betas only harden: guarantees, the review, translations, distribution |
| K2 | Exec: tier and session limits? | T2, operators and up, a step-up per shell, 15 minutes idle and 2 hours in all. Audit who and when, never keystrokes |
| K3 | Config in git: one way or two? | One way, hub to git, with restore. Two-way needs conflict rules and an apply step, and 1.0 doesn't need it |
| K4 | Prometheus: alerts only, or queries on tiles too? | Both: alerts as incidents are the reason to add it; a query tile is small once the client exists. Recording rules into history wait |
| K5 | Synology: which DSM? | DSM 7.2 and up, read only. DSM 6 is end of life |
| K6 | Feeds: who fetches? | The hub, like `ical`, with the same size and time limits. The browser never fetches a feed, so no third-party request leaves it |
| K7 | Display rotation: how does scope work across boards? | The union of the boards' scopes for the socket, each board's own for its tiles. A board added to a display shows in its audit |
| K8 | Home Assistant proposing actions: which token? | An agent token, so it's the agents' approval path exactly: proposals, never actions; a passkey to approve |
| K9 | Camera pictures on a display: allowed at all? | Yes, only for cameras on its boards, at the tile's size, at most every 10 seconds. A stolen tablet then shows what the wall already showed, and no more |

## Decisions

Settled by the maintainer on 2026-11-06: **K1–K9 all as recommended.**

1. **K1:** v0.6 is the last minor before 1.0.
2. **K2:** exec is T2, operators and up, a step-up per shell, 15 minutes idle and 2 hours in all; the audit records who and when, never keystrokes.
3. **K3:** config goes one way, hub to git, with restore.
4. **K4:** Prometheus brings Alertmanager's alerts as incidents and PromQL tiles; recording rules wait.
5. **K5:** Synology DSM 7.2 and up, read only.
6. **K6:** the hub fetches feeds, like `ical`.
7. **K7:** a rotating display's socket serves the union of its boards' scopes; each board's tiles see only their own.
8. **K8:** Home Assistant proposes with an agent token, through the agents' approval path.
9. **K9:** a display shows camera pictures only for cameras on its boards, at the tile's size, at most every 10 seconds.
