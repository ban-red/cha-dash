## Give it a place

Enter the place's latitude and longitude in decimal degrees, north and east positive. Any map shows them, for example from a right-click on the spot.

## Notes

- Cha Dash asks Open-Meteo every 15 minutes while someone watches and every 30 minutes otherwise, well within its free limits.
- The forecast data is CC BY 4.0; the widget credits Open-Meteo.
